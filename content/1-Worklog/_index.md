During my 11-week internship, from 01/06/2026 to 14/08/2026, I worked on **Developing a Web Application on the Cloud**. The main deliverable was Cloud E-Wallet, a simulated e-wallet built with React/TypeScript, Java Spring Boot, MySQL, and AWS services.

The first eight weeks covered analysis, development, testing, and deployment. Week 9 focused on product and report completion. During the final two weeks, I researched Amazon ECS and CI/CD as future improvements; neither was implemented in production.
