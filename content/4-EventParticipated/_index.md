During the internship, I participated in two activities organized by First Cloud AI Journey. Each provided a different experience: the AWS knowledge competition reinforced my Cloud foundation as I observed teams analyze questions and make decisions under time pressure, whereas the Agentic AI Build Week sharing session showed how teams define a problem, build an MVP, and communicate product value.

### [4.1. AWS Knowledge Competition for Interns](4.1-Event1/)

The competition was held on June 20, 2026, with eight intern teams. I attended as an audience member and observed how the teams analyzed AWS questions, discussed alternatives, and agreed on one answer within the time limit.

### [4.2. FCAJ Sharing Session – Agentic AI Build Week 2026](4.2-Event2/)

The July 25, 2026 session reviewed the journeys of four FCAJ teams at Agentic AI Build Week. Through their case studies and discussions, I learned about business-first thinking, MVP scope, team coordination, and pitching through practical value rather than a list of technologies.
